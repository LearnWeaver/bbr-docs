CAD model#

A STEP model of the Expander, for designing a mount, checking clearance around the connectors, or dropping the board into a robot assembly before you have the board in your hand.

Download the STEP model (ZIP, about 5 MB — the STEP inside is around 35 MB)

STEP (.step, ISO 10303) imports into every CAD package teams actually use — Onshape, Fusion, SolidWorks, Inventor, FreeCAD — with no conversion. Unzip it first; CAD tools want the .step itself, not the archive.

What it is for#

The model is a mechanical reference, not a manufacturing document. Use it to work out where the board goes and what will hit what:

  • Mounting positions and hole locations.
  • Clearance for the connectors and the cables that plug into them — measure against the model rather than the bare PCB outline, because a JST lead needs more room than the connector body does.
  • Interference checks against the rest of your robot.

Take dimensions from the model itself in your own CAD package. Numbers copied out of a document drift out of date; the model does not.

What it is not#

It does not describe the electronics. There is no schematic, no netlist and no board file here, and the geometry is not a substitute for the pinout — for which pin does what, see What you have and the wiring guide.

Component bodies in the model are representative shapes for fit checking. If you need a critical dimension for something that must not touch the board — a tolerance stack that ends in millimetres of margin — measure the real board as well.
